# Project Rubric

Status: draft for review.

Every project and capstone is assessed on the same five criteria. A project passes when every criterion is at **Meets** or above. One revision is allowed after feedback.

Projects are reviewed by two peers. Where the two reviews disagree on whether a criterion is met, the Program Director decides. Capstones are reviewed by the Program Director.

## Guidance for peer reviewers

- Score each criterion and give one specific reason for the score.
- Name one thing that works well and one concrete change that would improve the project.
- Review the work, not the person.

| Criterion | Not yet | Meets | Exceeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Question** | The question is vague or cannot be answered with the data | The question is specific, answerable, and matters to a named audience | The question is sharpened by what the analysis revealed, and alternatives are considered |
| **Data** | The source is missing, or quality problems are ignored | The source and license are stated; cleaning decisions and limitations are documented | Likely biases and their effect on the conclusions are examined |
| **Analysis** | Methods are wrong for the question, or results are not checked | Methods fit the question; uncertainty is reported; results are checked | Results are tested against alternative explanations or methods |
| **Communication** | Charts mislead or conclusions go beyond the evidence | Charts are honest and labeled; conclusions match the evidence; the recommendation is clear | A non-specialist could act on the work; limitations are stated plainly |
| **Reproducibility** | The work does not run, or steps are missing | The repository runs from start to finish with instructions; the AI-use log is complete (Stage 2 onward) | Others can easily reuse the work: clear structure, tests, pinned environment |

## Capstone additions

Capstones also include:

- A one-page decision memo for the named audience
- A 5-minute recorded walkthrough, explaining the question, the data, the key decisions, and the limits of the conclusions
