# Peer review guide

Peer review is how you get feedback quickly and how you learn to judge work, including your own. You will review two stories from your group, and two peers will review yours.

## How to review

1. Open your peer's repository (you will receive access) and read `story.ipynb` from top to bottom.
2. Open a new issue in their repository titled **Peer review by @your-username**.
3. Copy the template below into the issue and fill it in.
4. Submit within two days of receiving the assignment.

## Template

```markdown
**Question:** Is the question specific and answerable with this chart? Meets / Not yet
Reason:

**Data:** Are the source and license given, and are limits of the data mentioned? Meets / Not yet
Reason:

**Chart:** Does it have a title, axis labels, and units, and does it show the data honestly? Meets / Not yet
Reason:

**Story:** Do the claims match what the chart shows? Meets / Not yet
Reason:

**One thing that works well:**

**One concrete change that would improve the story:**
```

## Good reviews

- Are specific: "The y-axis has no units" helps; "the chart is unclear" does not.
- Point to evidence in the work.
- Suggest a change the author can make this week.
- Review the work, never the person.

If the two reviews of a story disagree on whether something meets the standard, the program lead decides.
