# Stage 0: Onboarding sprint (weeks 1 to 2)

Stage 0 gets you set up and publishing in two weeks. It also shows us, and you, that the program fits your life before the longer stages begin.

## What you do

| Week | Module | You submit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [0.1 Tools and ways of working](0.1-tools-and-ways-of-working.md) | The **tools check** assignment |
| 2 | [0.2 First data story](0.2-first-data-story.md) | The **first data story** assignment, and two peer reviews |

Both weeks: submit the weekly check-in (a two-minute form, linked in the forum) with your work on Friday.

Plan for 10 to 12 hours each week.

## How you know you have finished Stage 0

You finish Stage 0 when all four are true:

1. Every automatic check passes on your **tools check** repository.
2. Every automatic check passes on your **first data story** repository.
3. You have written two peer reviews, following the [peer review guide](peer-review.md).
4. You have submitted both weekly check-ins.

Fellows who finish Stage 0 are placed in a peer group and continue to Stage 1.

## Getting help

- **Automatic checks** tell you what is missing each time you push your work.
- **AI tools as tutors**: see the [AI tutor cards](ai-tutor-cards.md). In Stage 0, AI tools may explain and give hints but may not write your code or text. See the [AI-use policy](../../policies/ai-use.md).
- **Discussion forum**: ask questions where everyone can learn from the answers.
- **Weekly office hour** with the program lead, recorded for those who cannot attend.
