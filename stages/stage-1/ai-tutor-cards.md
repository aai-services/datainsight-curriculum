# AI tutor cards: Stage 1

In Stage 1, AI tools are tutors, not writers. Copy a prompt and fill in the brackets.

## Understanding a concept

> I am learning [pandas merge / the bootstrap / the interquartile range] in a data science course where I may not have code written for me. Explain it with one small example that is different from my assignment, then ask me a question to check I understood.

## Debugging

> Here is my error message and the cell that caused it: [paste]. Do not rewrite my code. Tell me which line is the problem and ask me a question that helps me fix it.

## Checking your reasoning

> I cleaned a dataset like this: [describe your steps in words]. What could go wrong with these decisions? List risks; do not write code.

## Interpreting results

> I computed a difference of [number] with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval of [low] to [high]. Here is my interpretation: [paste]. Is it correct? Point out any mistakes in my reasoning without rewriting it for me.

## Practising

> Give me three short exercises on [filtering rows in pandas / groupby / percentiles], one at a time, using a tiny made-up table. Wait for my answer before giving feedback.

Always check what an AI tool tells you: run the example yourself and compare it with the lesson.
