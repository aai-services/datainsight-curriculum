# Stage 1: Foundations (weeks 3 to 10)

Stage 1 builds the working skills every later stage depends on: Python, pandas, cleaning, combining tables, describing data, and measuring uncertainty. It ends with two projects on data from your own country or community.

In Stage 1, AI tools are **tutors only**: they may explain and give hints, but may not write your code or text. See the [AI-use policy](../../policies/ai-use.md) and the [AI tutor cards](ai-tutor-cards.md).

## A module week

1. Work through the lesson in Colab, running every cell. Change things and rerun them.
2. Complete that week's practice notebook until its check passes.
3. Join your peer group's check-in, and the office hour if you can.
4. On Friday, submit the weekly check-in.

## A project week

1. Read the brief and choose your data early in the week.
2. Submit by Friday; the automatic checks must pass.
3. Review two peers' projects within two days, using the [project rubric](../../rubrics/project-rubric.md) and the [peer review guide](../stage-0/peer-review.md).
4. Revise once based on your reviews.

## Finishing Stage 1

- All six practice checks pass
- Both projects meet the rubric standard on every criterion, allowing one revision
- At least 80 percent of weekly check-ins submitted

Milestone: Foundations badge.
