# Weekly check-in

Every Friday, with your week's work, fellows answer three short questions. It takes two minutes and is how the program lead spots fellows who need help early.

## Questions

Set these up as a free Tally form.

1. **Your GitHub username** (short text, required)
2. **Where are you stuck, if anywhere?** (long text, optional)
3. **How confident do you feel about this week's material?** (scale 1 to 5, required)
4. **About how many hours did you spend this week?** (number, required)

Question 1 identifies the fellow; questions 2 to 4 are the three check-in questions.

## What the program lead does with the answers

Once a week, sort the responses and act on:

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| No check-in and no submission for two weeks in a row | Send the check-in message below |
| Confidence 1 or 2 | Invite them to the office hour or suggest a forum post |
| A specific "stuck" answer shared by several fellows | Cover it in the next office hour |
| Hours well above 12 for most fellows | The week's workload is too heavy; trim it for the next cohort |
| Hours well below 8 for most fellows | Check whether the work is being skipped or is too light |

## Check-in message

> Hi @username, I noticed you have not submitted for the last two weeks. That happens, and it is fine to ask for help. Is something getting in the way? If now is not a good time, you can pause and rejoin the next cohort at the start of your current stage. Just reply to let me know.
